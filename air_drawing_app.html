NEON AIR LAB / 01

Draw in the air using your index finger. Pinch thumb and index finger together to draw; release to lift the brush.

  • 2D Canvas: draw on a flat digital canvas.
  • 3D Space: create depth-like strokes using estimated hand depth from apparent hand scale and landmark movement. A regular webcam does not provide reliable true depth.
  • Works best in a modern browser over HTTPS or localhost.

Hand tracking uses MediaPipe loaded from a CDN, so the first launch needs internet. Drawing and exporting work offline after the page loads, but tracking may require the model files to be cached.
